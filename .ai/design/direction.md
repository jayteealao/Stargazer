## Goals

- **Clear states.** Every screen tells "nothing here yet", "nothing matches" and "something failed" apart, and names the next action. Reason: the current single empty state confuses new users.
- **Accessibility.** Every control has a content description, no state is shown by color alone, and labels stay readable. Reason: the current icons and tag colors carry meaning without text.
- **Big screens.** Foldable and tablet layouts, for example a list-detail split. Reason: the app is single-column only.
- **Faster organizing.** Actions on the detail screen, quick filter chips, and grouping. Reason: organizing is the product's positioning.
